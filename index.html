Make yourself at home.

WASD or arrow keys to drive or walk. Drag the view to look around. Shift runs and Space jumps on foot; Space brakes in the car. E gets out of the car when stopped; return nearby to get in. C changes the camera. H honks. T speaks with a nearby pedestrian. P pauses. The map can be minimized or expanded. Drag the expanded map to move across Kothrud, Deccan, and FC Road, use the wheel to zoom, and RECENTER to follow yourself. Checkboxes hide roads, buildings, traffic, people, or places.

Visit the three amber markers on your evening run. Use “Return to Deccan” if you get stuck.
