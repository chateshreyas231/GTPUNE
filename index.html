WELCOME TO THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

Make yourself at home.

On foot, WASD walks relative to the camera and Shift runs. Click the street to look with the mouse; Esc releases the pointer. E enters the closest car within a few metres, including traffic, and exits again when you have nearly stopped. Q or the left mouse button throws a short punch. T talks. H honks. F handles the dabba delivery. P pauses. In a car, WASD drives and steers, Space brakes, and the mouse looks around without turning the wheel. C changes the driving camera. People on the footpath walk to a further spot, then pick another. Most of the moving traffic is motorcycles, and it continues through junctions.

Visit the three amber markers on your evening run. Use “Return to Deccan” if you get stuck.

Prototype: generated buildings, approximate streets and fictional residents. No claim of exact Pune geometry or real building likeness. Bundled Marathi, Hindi and Indian English neural speech plays after sound is enabled. Voices are synthetic. Building facades are inferred rather than surveyed.
