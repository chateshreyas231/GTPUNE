WELCOME TO THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

Make yourself at home.

WASD or arrow keys to drive or walk. On foot, moving the mouse looks around without holding a button, and walking follows that look. In the car the camera follows the car. Esc releases the pointer. Shift runs and Space jumps on foot; Space brakes in the car. E gets out of a stopped car, and gets into any car within 7 metres. V switches among the girl, the boy, and the man. C changes the camera. H honks. T speaks with a nearby pedestrian. F always punches or swings; G picks up weapons or handles deliveries; 1–4 selects fists, pipe, stick, or stone. Tilde opens cheats. P pauses. Cars stop for people. The map can be minimized or expanded. Drag the expanded map to move across Kothrud, Deccan, and FC Road, use the wheel to zoom, and RECENTER to follow yourself. Checkboxes hide roads, buildings, traffic, people, or places.

Visit the three amber markers on your evening run. Use “Return to Deccan” if you get stuck.

Prototype: generated buildings, approximate streets and fictional residents. No claim of exact Pune geometry or real building likeness. Bundled Marathi, Hindi and Indian English neural speech plays after sound is enabled. Voices are synthetic. Building facades are inferred rather than surveyed.
